## 🔬 Interests

Production-grade backend systems and developer tooling. I'm happiest writing Kotlin services that quietly do their job at 3am.

Self-hosting is the other hobby that ate my life: a DietPi homelab running Paperless-NGX, Linkwarden, n8n, and YouTrack, stitched together over Tailscale. There's a Pi cluster involved. There's always a Pi cluster involved.
